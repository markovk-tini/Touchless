YouTube, no remote, no mouse, no Alt-Tab.

Eating dinner. Cooking. Lifting weights. Holding a baby. Across the room. Hands dirty. Touchless lets you wave a video on, skip the ad, jump it to fullscreen — all without touching the keyboard or the trackpad. Works in any YouTube tab in any browser.

What you can do, hands-free

Twelve gestures and a vocabulary of voice commands, all wired to the YouTube player actions you actually use. No new shortcut keys to memorise — just say it or sign it.

Play / Pause

Wave to pause the cooking show

Open palm pauses, fist resumes. Or say "pause" from across the kitchen. No more wiping flour off your hands to tap the space bar.

Skip ads

Skip past pre-rolls without lifting a finger

Skip the ad button is one swipe right. Say "skip ad" and it's gone. Your sandwich stays in your hand.

Seek

Jump 10 seconds at a time

Swipe right to fast-forward, swipe left to rewind. Say "forward thirty" to jump half a minute. Tutorials and lectures stop being a scrubber game.

Fullscreen

Go fullscreen from the couch

Pinch outward to fullscreen, pinch in to come back. Or just say "fullscreen". Works whether the laptop's on your lap or six feet away.

Theater mode

Bigger video, same tab

A single gesture toggles YouTube's theater layout — wider video without the commitment of going fullscreen. Great for second-monitor watching while you work.

Volume

Turn it down without the cat hearing

Two fingers up = louder. Two fingers down = quieter. Or whisper "volume thirty" and Touchless lands it exactly there.

Mute

Instant mute when your name's called

One gesture cuts the audio. Same gesture brings it back. Faster than alt-tabbing to mute a tab, and it works from the other side of the room.

Next video

Skip the recap, hit the next episode

Swipe up to jump to the next video in the queue or autoplay list. Binge a playlist without ever touching the mouse.

Why this isn't a browser extension

Browser-extension YouTube remotes need permissions, slow your browser down, and only work in one tab at a time. Touchless runs as a standalone Windows app — it watches your hands through the webcam and your voice through the microphone, and translates them into the same keystrokes you'd press yourself. YouTube can't tell the difference. Neither can the next site you visit.

That means no extension to install, no permissions to grant in your browser, no conflicts with whatever ad-blocker or theme you're already running. It just works, every YouTube tab, every browser.

Try it on your next YouTube session

Install Touchless once. It starts when you sign in, sits quietly in the tray, and lights up the moment your hand moves. Camera and microphone permissions are requested up-front and never sent anywhere — every model runs on your machine.
