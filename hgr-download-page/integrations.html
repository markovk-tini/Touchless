Apps Touchless plays nicely with

Integrations

Touchless can drive a handful of other apps directly — by gesture or by voice. Here's the list.

Available now

Spotify
● Live

Play, pause, skip, change volume, and switch playlists — by gesture or voice.
YouTube
● Live

Play, pause, seek, fullscreen, and theater-mode toggle in any YouTube tab.

Coming soon…

Discord
Coming soon

Mute, deafen, and switch voice channels by gesture or voice. Coming in a future Touchless update.

Apple Music
Coming soon

Play, pause, skip, and change volume on your Apple Music library.

Want a new integration?

If there's an app you'd really like Touchless to control — and it has an official, free API — open an issue or email konstantinvmarkov@gmail.com. A name, a link to the app's developer docs, and one sentence about what you'd want to control is enough.
