Touchless

Control your PC with hand gestures and your voice. No touch required. Live camera tracking, voice commands, and dictation all run locally on your machine.

Checking your device…

Hand-gesture control

Static poses and motion gestures map to actions in real time. Hold, swipe, pinch, or pose — Touchless reads your hand straight from your webcam.

Voice commands & dictation beta

Speak naturally to open apps, play media, or dictate text into any window. Voice transcription runs locally with whisper.cpp — no cloud round-trip. Dictation is in beta — accuracy and latency are still being tuned.

Mouse mode

Move the cursor with an open palm, pinch to click, and scroll by lifting two fingers. Toggle on and off with a left-hand pose; no extra hardware needed.

Drawing & capture

Draw on top of your screen, capture screenshots, record clips — all triggered by gesture or voice. Save where you want.

Local by default

Camera frames, microphone audio, and recognized gestures stay on your device. No account, no telemetry of personal content.

GPU-accelerated

Voice transcription and grammar correction run on CUDA, Vulkan, or your CPU as a fallback. Touchless picks the fastest backend automatically.

App integrations

Spotify and YouTube are already wired up — play, pause, skip, change volume, switch playlists by gesture or voice. Discord is on the roadmap.
