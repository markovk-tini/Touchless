1. **Scenario** — what is happening
2. **Who it's for** — who should feel this was made for them
3. **How to post** — cut, cover, caption, tags, day
4. **Script** — picture, action, spoken line, on-screen text, timed

Gesture + voice only. No Iris. Overlay is on from **0:00**. Lines in
`[brackets]` are silent — the hand is the line.

Exact voice commands when a script uses them:

- `Open touchless clip`

## Locked situations — film these, not a vibe

One place, one missed cue, one reason a stranger sends it. Do not
improvise a “generic walking around” take. If the cue is a one-shot
voice line, it will be over before the fist lands — use **continuous**
sound unless the card says otherwise.

G2 vs G3 vs G6 vs G8 (do not mash these):

| ID | You still want music? | What dies | Missed cue |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G2** | No, not right now | Spotify pauses. Game audio stays. | World mix (footsteps, town, a nearby enemy) buried under the track |
| **G3** | Yes, just quieter | Only the **Spotify** volume bar | Same footsteps, but the song stays under them |
| **G6** | Yes in buy, no in round | Spotify pauses on the round-start cue | Ranked footsteps / abilities / callouts |

### G1 — Skip mid-glide

**Game:** Genshin Impact.  
**Where:** A long glide you cannot fail. Best: Starsnatch Cliff → beach,
or any high Fontaine / Mondstadt launch into open sky. Not a combat
glide. Not a 3-second hop.  
**On screen:** Sky, character in air, stamina bar fine. Mini live view
parked top-left.  
**What’s wrong:** Spotify shuffled into something that **cannot** belong
on that shot — a podcast, a gym countdown, Christmas music, a talking
comedy bit. Let it play for a full second.  
**The fix:** Swipe left. A track that actually fits the sky. You never
look down. You never stop flying.  
**Why it is relatable:** Every open-world player has had shuffle ruin a
vista, then taken a hand off WASD to skip and drifted into a tree. A
glide is the one moment you **cannot** look at the keyboard. That is
the joke.  
**Start / end:** Already in the air / still in the air.  
**Kill the take if:** you land, alt-tab, they cannot hear song A vs B.

### G2 — Fist pause (the “I missed that” clip)

**Game:** Genshin Impact (backup: Hogwarts Legacy courtyard).  
**Where:** Walking, not standing. Mondstadt cobblestones at dusk, or
Liyue Harbor boards. Hard ground so footsteps exist. After the fist you
**keep walking** so nobody thinks you paused the game.  
**The cue you are missing:** Not a 1-second NPC line — it will be over.
Use something that **keeps making sound**:

1. **Best, easiest:** your own footsteps + town/wind, buried under a
   loud drop or a talking podcast. After the fist: boots and world, no
   song.
2. **Stronger if you can stage it:** walk toward a **Seelie** (constant
   chime) or idle **Hilichurls** (they mutter on a loop). After pause,
   the chime or growl is obvious.

**What you are not missing:** a ranked footstep. That is G6.  
**Why it is relatable:** Anyone who games with Spotify has done this:
the playlist is so loud you miss the world, you stab at media keys or
alt-tab, and you walk off a cliff or into a fight. This clip is that
feeling **without** dying on camera. The punchline is: music gone,
character still moving — so they know you paused Spotify, not Genshin.  
**Spoken problem:** “I can’t hear the game.” That is the cue. Do not
say “pause.”  
**Start / end:** Walking + buried mix / walking + game-only mix.  
**Kill the take if:** you use the mute pose (that kills game audio
too), the character stands still, or Spotify does not actually pause.

## How they know (read this before filming G1–G3)

A stranger will not take your word for it. “Wrong song” and “too loud”
are claims. They agree only if they can **check** the claim in one
second — with sound **or** with their eyes if sound is off.

**1. They have to hear Spotify.**  
If you game on headphones, the Reel is silent and the joke dies. For
the take, play Spotify on **speakers**, or record system audio in OBS
and lay it under the phone clip. The skip, the pause, the volume drop
must be audible on Instagram.

**2. Muted viewers need a chip.**  
Burn in a now-playing line that **changes**.

- Skip: `Now playing: Tax Office Jazz` → `Now playing: [the good one]`
- Pause: chip gains a pause bar, or `Playing` → `Paused`
- Volume: the Touchless **Spotify** bar visibly drops; game bar stays

Do not cut to a full Spotify window. A lower-third chip, or a tiny
Spotify mini-player already on the monitor, is enough.

**3. Pick a mismatch they already believe.**  
“Wrong” is not a genre. Wrong is **this vista + that sound**.

| You want them to feel | Put this on the glide / walk | After the gesture |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong song | A talking podcast, a gym countdown, Christmas music, a comedy bit | A track that actually fits the sky |
| Too loud | A Genshin voice line or footstep **buried** under the drop | Same SFX, suddenly clear |
| Paused | Music blasting | Music gone, character still walking |
| Volume on Spotify only | Dual bar: Spotify high, game already fine | Only the Spotify bar moves |

**4. Say the problem they can verify, not the feature.**  
Don’t announce “I will now skip.” React like a person.

| Don’t say | Say |
|---|---|
| Wrong song. | This is not a glide song. / Absolutely not. |
| Music's too loud. | I can't hear the game. / I missed that. |
| I don't want the game quiet. | I can't hear the footsteps. |
| Didn't even look down. | There we go. (after they heard the new track) |

The line names what they just heard or are about to hear. The hand
does the how.

**5. One second of problem, then the fix.**
