## 1. What is live vs later

### Film this week

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Instant clip (last 30s–1 min, no dialog) | Left-hand two, or left-one then **“clip that”** / **“clip the last 30 seconds”** |
| Open clips folder | **“Open touchless clip”** |
| Pick a clip | **“Open touchless clip file”** (newest on top) |
| Pause / play Spotify (and other media) | Right fist |
| Next / previous track | Swipe left / swipe right |
| Spotify volume (game can stay up) | Volume pose; lean onto the **Spotify** bar if dual-bar is showing |
| Everything silent | Shaka mute pose = **system** mute (game + Spotify both die) |
| Drawing | Left-hand four |
| Air mouse | Left-hand three (palm move, pinch click, two-finger scroll) |
| Screenshot | Screen-wheel pose |
| Open / focus Spotify | Right-hand two (Spotify **Premium** required) |
| Voice play a song | Left-one, then **“play [song] on Spotify”** |
| Phone as camera | Settings → Camera → QR |

Instant clip must be enabled under Save Locations. Default duration is 1 minute; set **30 seconds** for filming. Games in **borderless / windowed fullscreen**. Turn **Gaming Overlay auto-hide off** so the volume bar and clip toast show on camera.

### Do not claim as shipped

- Native Discord mute / deafen / channel switch (website: coming soon)
- Native Zoom / Teams mute APIs
- “Mute Discord with a fist” as a first-class feature
- macOS
- Work / Game / Stream profiles as a finished public story until the UI is copyable by a stranger

The mute pose is **system-wide**. To drop only music: fist = pause, or volume pose on the Spotify bar.

## 2. How Instagram ranks in 2026

Instagram is four systems, not one: **Reels** find strangers, Feed mixes both, Stories talk to followers, Explore is almost all non-followers.

Named levers (Mosseri): **watch time**, likes per reach (followers), **sends per reach** (strangers). A DM send is treated like a personal recommendation. Public creator writeups: ~**1–2% sends/reach** is strong, **3%+** is viral-tier.

Under ~50k followers, Reels are the discovery engine (one 2026 study: ~31% reach rate, ~55% of views from non-followers). Carousels win saves later.

**Trial Reels** (non-follower-only tests) usually need a public Creator/Business account around **1,000 followers**. Until then, the “short clip for strangers” lane is a normal Reel cut to **7–15 seconds**. After 1k, compare trial-to-trial, then graduate winners.

April 2026: accounts that **mostly repost** lose recommendation to non-followers. Hashtags capped at **5**; they do **not** buy reach. Keywords in the name field, first caption line, on-screen text, and alt text do. No TikTok watermark. No bought likes (signals are ratios).

Stay under **90 seconds** or you fall out of the Reels tab.

## 3. Three audiences

**Gamer / clipper / streamer is one audience.** Clipping is a feature. Streaming is the same person on a different day. Instagram would keep showing you to the same cluster.

Three audiences that sit in **different recommendation graphs**:

| | Who | First frame | Send-to line | Test mix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. PC gamers** | Play on Windows. Music on. Sometimes clip. A streamer who is just playing sits here. | A game they recognize | Send this to anyone who games with music on | 40% |
| **2. Screen explainers** | Tutors, teachers, coaches, “circle this.” Reviewing a play is this job, not a third gamer type. | A line on the screen | Send this to anyone who explains things on a screen | 30% |
| **3. Lean-back Windows** | Laptop on couch/bed. No mouse nearby. Lectures, YouTube, Spotify, browsing. | Empty desk, phone, browser | Send this to anyone who uses a laptop in bed | 30% |

A streamer who is just playing is **1**. A streamer who is marking a replay is **2**. Do not build a third gamer lane.

After **12 Reels**, keep the two with the best sends per reach and % non-follower views. Kill the third for three weeks, then retest one clip. Do not mash all three into one “gaming lifestyle” grid.

## 4. Which games to film

Instagram Reels in 2026 prefer pretty, sendable, self-contained clips. Q2 2026 game-brand Instagram interactions: **Fortnite, then Genshin, then Minecraft**. Genshin did that with ~4.5M followers vs Fortnite’s ~27M. Reels over-index on aesthetic / narrative. Competitive shooters still win **clip culture** because a round is readable in two seconds. Party “friendslop” (Meccha Chameleon) gets DMs.

| Game | Use it for | When in the session | Download? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Genshin Impact** | Music, volume, screenshots, air mouse on the map | Exploration, glide, boat, teapot, new-region walking. Not a first-try weekly boss. | You have it. Primary music set. |
| **Hogwarts Legacy** | Aesthetic Reels, screenshots, castle walking, drawing on a vista | Night castle, broom travel, common room. Not a story dungeon you have not seen. | You have it. Primary pretty set. |
| **Valorant** | Instant clip. Round structure is readable to non-players. | Clip on the clutch. Music only in agent select / buy / post-round. Never mid-round. | Keep. Test overlay vs Vanguard once before a shoot day. |
| **Meccha Chameleon** | Hide-prep music, freeze, clip the find, draw on the replay | Paint/prep phase, then freeze. Seekers: clip the reveal. | You have it. Best “send to the group chat” game. |
| **Fortnite (free)** or **Warzone** | Extra clip recognition if Valorant overlay is painful | Same as Valorant: clip the moment, music only in queue/drop | Fortnite over CoD or Halo. |
| **Halo / paid CoD** | — | — | Skip for reach. |

**Valorant:** test one session in borderless before a shoot day. If Vanguard fights overlays, Genshin + Hogwarts + Meccha are enough for a month.

**Music belongs only when a stranger can tell you are still playing:**

- Genshin: glide, walk, boat, teapot, city. Not a hard boss.
- Hogwarts: night courtyards, known broom route, common room.
- Valorant: buy, queue, post-round. Fist-pause when the round *starts*.
- Meccha: music during paint/prep; fist-pause when you freeze.

## 5. Profile as a destination

Treat the profile as a landing page.

- **Name field (searchable):** `Touchless | Gesture + Voice for Windows`

- **Category:** Productivity. Creator or Business account.
- **Highlights (5, one word):** Start · Game · Draw · Couch · Setup
- **Pin 3:** (1) hand controls the PC, (2) current winning audience’s best send, (3) how to download + Windows-only so Mac users do not bounce angry

Grid: top nine should look like one product. Alternate a vertical still from a Reel, a carousel cover, and a dark UI screenshot. No random memes.

Stories every day (5–8 frames) convert profile visits into follows. They do not grow the account.

## 7. Camera recipe

**Default:** one phone, over your shoulder, 9:16. Monitor fills most of the frame. Your **real hand** in the lower third. **No facecam.** If you need the skeleton, put Touchless’s **mini live view on the monitor** so the phone records it in the same take.

Never facecam + live view + game as three equal panels.

| If the joke is… | Facecam | Mini live view on the monitor | Phone |
|---|---|---|---|
| The hand (skip, pause, clip, volume, draw) | Off. Face steals the lower third from the hand. | Small PiP, skeleton on, ~18% of frame, opposite the hand | Over-shoulder, 9:16, hand in lower third |
| A spoken line (“clip that”, “play X”) | Optional tiny PiP only if the mouth helps. Captions required either way. | Keep the small skeleton PiP so it does not look like a hidden keyboard | Same over-shoulder, or couch wide enough to see laptop + hand |
| The pretty picture (Hogwarts / Genshin vista) | Off | Off until the gesture. Then 1 second of PiP or just the real hand | Monitor-fill. Hand enters from the bottom for the pose, then leaves |

**Once per shoot:** 1080×1920. Captions burned in. No logo sting. Original audio. Hide Discord overlay.

## 12. What not to do

**Kills non-follower reach:** reposted clips, CapCut/TikTok watermarks, 30-hashtag walls, engagement bait (“comment YES”), bought likes/views, the same Reel uploaded twice, AI talking-head with no real demo, feature-list voiceovers, 3-minute tutorials as discovery.

**Kills trust for this product:** claiming it works on Mac; claiming native Discord is live; medical RSI cure claims; accessibility inspiration content without that community; showing a crashy install; slow logo intro; asking people to DM “LINK” before the profile is clear.

**Do not film Discord mute.**

## 13. Stolen viral clips

Putting a facecam on someone else’s viral clip does **not** make it yours and is usually **not legal**. A face, border, or caption is not meaningful transformation. Instagram’s 2026 originality rule also throttles aggregator accounts.

Film **your** gameplay. Copy pacing (hook in second 1, captions, zoom on the hand), not the file.

Your own Genshin / Valorant / Hogwarts capture + your hand is the legal and on-strategy path. Follow each game’s video policy; blur other players’ names.

This is not legal advice.

## 14. Scoreboard

Track weekly, not empty view counts.

| Metric | Why | Act if |
|---|---|---|
| Sends / reach | Unconnected-reach lever | Below your own median 3 times: rewrite the hook and the send-to line |
| % non-follower views | Reels should be mostly strangers while small | Under 40%: shorter, colder, more specific in line 1 |
| Profile visits → follows | Reel worked; profile leaked. Small accounts: roughly 8–20% | Visits up, follows flat: bio, pins, Highlights |
