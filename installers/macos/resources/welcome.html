Welcome to Touchless

Touchless lets you control your Mac with hand gestures and voice.

This installer places Touchless.app in your Applications folder.

After installing, the first launch will ask for a few macOS permissions (Camera, Microphone, and — for cursor/keyboard control — Accessibility and Screen Recording). Touchless can't grant these for you; it will guide you to System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later.
