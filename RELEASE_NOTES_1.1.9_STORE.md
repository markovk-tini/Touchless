## Touchless 1.1.9 — What's new

**Updates**
- The in-app update prompt is visible again (it was hidden or crashed in 1.1.7–1.1.8.1)
- Download Update works for Store installs; a tray balloon is the backup if the popup is behind another window

**Custom gestures**
- Motion gestures (waves, circles, long paths) detect more reliably
- New pose-sequence gestures (hold 3 → 2 → 1, and similar)
- Preview the recorded path after you save a motion gesture

**Tracking**
- GPU-mode tracking recovers the hand more cleanly after it leaves the frame
